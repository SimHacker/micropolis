Physical rewards should require contributions of 8 times the cost 
of the reward plus international shipping.

====

- Your Name or Alias in the Credits and Game
  - The game has a roster of citizen names, who can send letters 
    to the mayor complaining about problems, etc.

- Community Web Site Early Access
  - All of the above plus:
  - Early access to community web site.
  - Communicate with developers.
  - Vote on goals and features.
  - Play beta versions of the game.

- 1 Year Membership
  - All of the above plus:
  - One year membership in the community.

- Lifetime Membership
  - All of the above plus:
  - Lifetime membership in the community.

- Mayor/Governor/President/Ambassador Status
  - All of the above plus:
  - Special privileges on the community site. (what?)

- The following physical rewards must be priced high enough to cover 
  cost, shipping and labor:

- Box
  - An empty box, like you would see on the shelf of a software store, 
    with artwork on the outside describing the game that's not inside.

- Stickers
  - Stickers with buildings and tiles.

- Refrigerator Magnets
  - Magnets with buildings and tiles.
  - http://www.fridgedoor.com/custom-magnet-word-poetry-sets.html
  - http://www.fridgedoor.com/custom-magnet-word-set-poetry.html

- Poster
  - Big poster with a picture of a city.
  - $50

- Picture T-Shirt
  - T-shirt with a picture of a city on the front.

- All-over T-Shirt
  - T-shirt with a city printed all over it.

- Other Merchandise: Mouse Pad, Pillow, etc.

- Original Design Documents
  - Limited number: 50.
  - Framed and signed.
  - $500

- The following rewards depend on the 3D city goal being reached:

- 3D Printed Building/City
  - If 3D city goal is reached.

- Put Your Own 3D Building in the Game (Minecraft, Lego, etc)
  - If 3D city goal is reached.
