Integrate with Google Docs:

Export data to Google Sheets.

Trigger and log events in Google Calendar.

Export graphs, visualizations and reports to Google Draw, Google Slides, Google Docs.

Google Forms for configuring and controlling simulation and scenarios.

Street view links. 

  Embed a Google Street View panorame in a location on a SimCity map, that illustrates what it looks like.
    (rotated to align with the SimCity coordinate system)

  Render "street view" panoramic images from 3d lego models, to embed and view in Google Maps.

  Drive a virtual Google Mapping car around your simcity map to build a digital map and render 3d streetview images.

  Represent streets as vectors in a map overlay, that drive the street building (and repairing) tools, 
  and associate metadata like street name, house numbers, intersections, etc with the street. 

  Declarative building tools. Create "blueprint layer" map overlays
  that declare plans to build named streets, zones and buildings
  beforehand, wait for approval and funding, then build over time,
  incrementally consume budget and resources, pause when unfunded or
  put on hold, repair after disasters, and permanently represent
  properties in the city.

  Copy and paste and edit these "blueprints" from map to map. 

  The building tools will create objects representing the things
  you're building, which are saved as overlays on map. They can have
  names, descriptions, stories, discussion forums, metadata, state,
  plug-in extensions, user interfaces, micro-simulators, etc.

  When you create a plan, it goes into the calendar on its creation
  date, and also on its construction milestones. 

  Events that happen in the simulation can publish stores and data
  into the calendar in a newspaper channel (or tv news show). 

  Use the calendar to schedule things in the future, and record things
  as they happen.
